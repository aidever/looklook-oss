Looklook desktop 1.82.0: the Assistant is now your home, above your sessions; the session list footer is a single row; the Assistant hands out work more safely, without overstepping or sending your words to the wrong place.

- Looklook opens on the Assistant (when it's on); clicking the Looklook logo brings you back to it.
- A fixed Assistant row at the top of the session list shows whether it is thinking, how much work is in progress and how much is waiting for you. Sessions grouped by project follow below and still open and take messages directly.
- On phones the bottom bar is Assistant · Sessions · + · Me.
- The session list footer is one row: your avatar (Tasks, History, Tools, AI and account), connection status and Settings. No more horizontal scrollbar in the session list.
- When the Assistant wants to give a new session more permission than it has itself (say, "let it go ahead"), you approve it first; with Codex as the Assistant the session starts at the Assistant's own level.
- The Assistant's background helpers never go beyond "ask first", even when the Assistant is set to go ahead.
- When the Assistant follows up with a session after reporting a result, it no longer passes along something you said later about another task.
- Work you handed over in Feishu reports back to that message, not to whichever group you spoke in last; work handed over in Looklook isn't pushed to Feishu.
- Once a piece of work is done and reported, the Assistant stops following that session, so using it yourself afterwards doesn't wake the Assistant.
- Results that fail to reach the Assistant are retried; queued sessions no longer get dropped when another one is queued at the same time; on computers with only Codex installed, work goes to Codex by default.
