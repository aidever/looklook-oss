Looklook desktop 1.83.0: the assistant now remembers.

- Tell the assistant "call me Wang" or "I use pnpm", or correct it once, and it remembers — even across new or compacted conversations. A "Remembered: …" line appears in the chat; tap it to see what was saved.
- A new Memory tab on the assistant page groups memories into About you, Corrections, Projects and Notes. You can add, edit, pin (always included; the assistant can't change it) and forget them (forgotten ones go to an archive you can restore from), and undo recent changes. Changes you make there reach the assistant on your next message.
- The assistant only remembers what you said. It won't store keys or passwords, and something it read on a web page never becomes one of your preferences.
- Memories stay on this computer, one file each, readable without Looklook.
