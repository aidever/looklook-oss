Looklook desktop 1.79.1: answering questions and approvals gives instant feedback, new Codex chats can ask you questions, and connection notices no longer get stuck.

- After you answer a question or tap Approve/Deny, the buttons lock right away and show "Sent", so a double tap can't send twice (in chat, in terminal choice dialogs and on the task board).
- Codex: new chats can show the question box from the very first turn; questions that don't allow a custom answer no longer offer one, and timed questions show a countdown.
- When opened remotely, the "key presses weren't sent" notice could stay on screen until you refreshed. It now closes after 10 seconds, shows its full text, and can be closed by tapping × or the notice itself; "Reconnected" closes after 2 seconds.
