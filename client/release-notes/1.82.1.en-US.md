Looklook desktop 1.82.1: the Me menu is redesigned and is no longer one long list.

- A profile card at the top shows your name and the days left on your membership (tap to open Membership & invites); tap your personal address to copy it.
- Tasks, History, Files, SSH, Pages, System, AI and Computers sit in a grid of icons.
- Below are just three rows: Membership & invites, Account & security and Settings. Language, Getting started, Keyboard shortcuts, Report a problem, Looklook home page, Help & docs, GitHub and Sign out are a single row of small links at the bottom.
- When you are not signed in, the card shows a button to turn on remote access.
