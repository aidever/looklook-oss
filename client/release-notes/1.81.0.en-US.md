Looklook desktop 1.81.0: the app now centers on sessions — the session list stays on every page and tools open over your session; the Claude account and provider you pick in Looklook are no longer overridden by CC Switch; context size is shown correctly; pets can have several sets of lines.

- The session list is on every page and holds the navigation: Assistant and New session at the top, Tasks, History, Tools, AI and Settings at the bottom. Collapse it to a narrow icon bar.
- Files, SSH, Web pages, System and AI settings slide in from the right over your session; close them and you are back where you were.
- New Web tab in each session: web servers running in that project (dev servers, previews…), opened here directly or turned into a page you can open from anywhere.
- Opening Looklook takes you back to your last session; New session still starts a new one.
- On phones the bottom bar is just Sessions · + · Me, with a count of sessions waiting for you; Tasks, History and Tools are under Me, and the Assistant sits at the top right.
- If you use CC Switch or similar tools: the Claude account, third-party provider and model you choose in Looklook now always apply. Sessions where you didn't pick one in Looklook still follow your own settings.
- When the AI asks a question, tapping "type your own" on a single-choice question clears the picked option, and picking an option again drops the typed text. On multiple-choice questions both are sent, as in Claude Code.
- Context size: uses what Claude Code / Codex report, remembered across restarts; before that it estimates with their own rules and says so (third-party models, the 1M-context switch and Looklook's model settings included). When unknown it shows only what's used instead of assuming 200k.
- Pet lines: pick which Claude Code account or Codex profile generates them (fixes "Not logged in"); pet packs can ship several styles of lines to choose from in Settings; export and import one or several sets; importing a pet from another computer brings its lines along; generating or importing always adds a new set and never overwrites the pack's own.
